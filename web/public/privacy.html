Privacy Policy

Effective 10 October 2026

Pumpfun Ads (pumpfunads.com, "Pumpfun Ads", "we") is a launchpad for coins on Pumpfun. Creator fees from each coin pay for marketing content that we make and post about it. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Your wallet address. You sign in by signing a message with your Solana wallet. We store the address and a session cookie. We never see or store your private keys.
  • What you submit when you launch a coin: its name, ticker, description, logo, the brief (pitch, style, audience, look) and any website or X link. This becomes public on the blockchain, on Pumpfun and on our site.
  • Accounts you link. If you link X or TikTok to your coin, we receive an access token for that account, along with its handle and account ID, under the permissions you approve. TikTok permissions: user.info.basic and video.upload. Tokens are stored encrypted.
  • Technical data. Your IP address and basic request information, used to limit abuse (rate limits) and kept in short-lived server logs.

We do not use advertising trackers, analytics services or third-party cookies.

How we use it

  • To sign you in, prepare your launch transaction and show your coins under "My coins".
  • To make content for your coin (images, videos, captions and a site) from your brief.
  • To post that content on our own accounts (@pumpfunads on X, @pumpfunads on TikTok). If you linked your own accounts, we also use them as described below.
  • To keep the service safe: rate limits, abuse prevention and fixing errors.

Linked TikTok and X accounts

  • TikTok: we use your token only to read your basic profile (to show which account is linked) and to upload videos made for your coin to your TikTok inbox as drafts. Nothing is published on your account unless you open the draft in TikTok and post it yourself.
  • X: we use your token only to post the content made for your coin, which you can see on your coin's page.
  • We do not read your messages, followers, contacts or other content, and we never sell or share your tokens.
  • You can unlink an account at any time on your coin's page, which deletes its token from our systems. You can also revoke access in your TikTok or X settings.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data. We share only what each service needs to do its job:

  • Higgsfield: generates images and videos from your brief.
  • Anthropic (Claude), directly or through OpenRouter: writes captions and scripts from your brief and checks content.
  • Pumpfun: stores your coin's metadata and logo (on IPFS) and creates the coin.
  • Solana RPC providers: send and read transactions. Price data comes from public market APIs.
  • X and TikTok: receive the content we post or upload as drafts.

Anything written to the Solana blockchain (your wallet address, your coin and its transactions) is public and permanent. No one, including us, can delete it.

How long we keep it

We keep launch records and generated content for as long as the coin is listed on Pumpfun Ads. Access tokens are deleted when you unlink an account. Server logs are kept for a short time and then removed.

Your choices

  • Unlink X or TikTok at any time.
  • Ask us to remove your coin's page and its content from our site, or to delete the data we hold about you. We cannot change anything already on the blockchain or already posted publicly by you.

Children

Pumpfun Ads is not for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
